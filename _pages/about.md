Hi, I'm Daisy. I'm a Lead Biostatistician in the [Department of Quantitative Health Sciences](https://www.lerner.ccf.org/quantitative-health/) at [Cleveland Clinic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cleveland_Clinic), a major academic medical center and health system in the United States. 

For the past eight years, I collaborate closely with orthopedic surgeons and clinical researchers to develop inferential and predictive models that support clinical decision-making and identify factors associated with patient-reported outcomes and healthcare utilization. More recently, my research has expanded into AI for Health, where I develop machine learning methods for integrating heterogeneous clinical data, including but not limited to medical imaging, clinical notes, and electronic health records, to learn robust representations and improve prediction of clinically meaningful outcomes.

To date, I have authored more than 75 peer-reviewed publications, with nearly 1,000 citations. My research has had broad impact beyond academia. My co-authored work has informed [U.S.](https://acrjournals.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/acr.25045?casa_token=b7xtmAONRTIAAAAA%3AJLHl8UvGj0eamkBVWnSu3h9GcnWPOazyBuKbJXh18pRUF8PhHqTIAuH8v5oN0wfyzXyO169cU1z6Muaz) and [international](https://ojs.reumatologia.org.ar/index.php/revistaSAR/article/view/773) clinical guidelines and recommendations, been highlighted in annual ["What’s New"](https://www.ovid.com/jnls/jbjsjournal/fulltext/10.2106/jbjs.20.01510~whats-new-in-musculoskeletal-tumor-surgery?casa_token=rnUUvBEnMpwAAAAA%3A_xlFYN979_VtIKu6JZEW7T4K9EpnjIhDd0Avai3rtXT-Rd35DEXeLL4TCZw_8uOqWFSYNIatR6RkGdWmG7hIlLlY) articles, and featured on [podcasts](https://orthojoe.castos.com/episodes/hot-topics-in-orthopaedics-the-impact-of-surgeon-variability-on-patient-outcomes) from leading journals and in [news media](https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2023/10/231012111701.htm). My first-author research on risk prediction models has been published as [commentaries](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0895435623000768) and [special feature](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0012369223009455?casa_token=1KXvKxYnslkAAAAA:xJAcy1AphuTxkwA2T8qm-KeLALlGvduMO63RgKtY3yAmgzgHco22GBc69tKU1fdJyzyG9j5US1Q) articles in leading journals in the field, including work recognized as an [Editors’ Choice article](https://www.jclinepi.com/article/S0895-4356(23)00196-8/fulltext).

In addition to my research, I serve on the [Editorial Board](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/chest/about/editorial-board) of *CHEST* and as a peer-reviewer for more than 10 journals and conferences, including International Conference on Machine Learning [(ICML)](https://icml.cc/), Machine Learning for Healthcare Conference [(MLHC)](https://mlhc.org/), *Arthritis Care & Research*, *Scientific Reports*. I also regularly present my work at statistical, computer science and clinical conferences, including MLHC, Conference on Statistical Practice [(CSP)](https://ww2.amstat.org/meetings/csp/2023/), annual meetings of the American College of Chest Physicians [(CHEST)](https://journal.chestnet.org/article/S0012-3692(22)03810-7/fulltext), International Society for Clinical Biostatistics [(ISCB)](https://communities.isi-web.org/event-iscb2024), and American Academy of Orthopaedic Surgeons [(AAOS)](https://www.aaos.org/annual/). I received the [Lester R Curtin Award](https://www.amstat.org/your-career/awards/lester-r-curtin-award) from the American Statistical Association in 2023. 

My academic training spans biology, biostatistics, and computer science. I received my B.S. in Biology with a minor in French from [UCLA](https://ucla.edu), my M.S. in Biostatistics from [Washington University in St Louis](https://i2db.wustl.edu/educationprograms/master-of-science-in-biostatistics-and-data-science/), and my Master of Computer and Information Technology from [University of Pennsylvania](https://online.engineering.upenn.edu/degrees/mcit-online/).
